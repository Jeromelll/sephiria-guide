Sephiria Wishing Fountain

For sephiria wishing fountain searches. Town pre-run picker—not the dungeon color-water fountain.

Players search sephiria wishing fountain because the town fountain is easy to miss: it is a pre-run Artifact picker, not the dungeon interactable whose water changes color.

What it does

Once unlocked, you select Artifacts in Bunnyville and carry those picks into the next dungeon run. Intro footage treats this as the “wishing well” step after you decide a weapon / combo family.

How you unlock it

Open it through New Events on Destiny Inscription—the same early spends that unlock the costume salon and extra weapons. See the beginner guide.

Capacity

  • Default capacity is 4 (Treyex talent writeup).
  • Willpower expands it: +1 at talent Lv5 and +4 at Lv20, for a writeup maximum of 9. A 1.0 tips video also treats Willpower as the stat that changes fountain / dice quality.
  • Higher rarity costs more capacity (Treyex’s bars: Common 3 / Advanced 4 / Rare 5 / Legendary 6). Confirm the current bars in-client—patch numbers move.
Do not mix this up with in-dungeon fountains that can grant XP, gold, or spawn enemies when the water changes color. Those are a different object.
