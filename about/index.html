About this site

Unofficial fan guide · Not affiliated with Team Horay

sephiria-guide.com is an unofficial English fan guide for Sephiria (Team Horay). It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by the developer.

Pages are short answers to real search questions (Hard Mode / Root’s Retreat, Destiny Inscription, builds, bosses). Facts are checked against Steam, community guides, and cross-sourced videos. Early Access–era numbers are marked when used.

Other unofficial Steam guides we maintain: How to Fish game guides (physics fishing co-op).
