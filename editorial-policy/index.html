Editorial policy

Last updated: 2026-08-21

sephiria-guide.com publishes short English answers to real Sephiria search questions. It is an unofficial fan site and is not affiliated with Team Horay.

Sources

  • Prefer Steam (store, news, discussions, achievements), dated community guides, and cross-checked videos.
  • P0 pages should cite at least two independent sources when a claim is contested.
  • Early Access–era numbers and outdated paths are marked when used. Unconfirmed details are labeled for in-game verification rather than invented.

Page scope

  • One page answers one clear player question (or a tightly related cluster). We do not pad thin long-tails without search evidence.
  • UI polish is secondary to correct Title / Description / H1 and a usable answer.

Updates and corrections

We revise pages when patches move unlocks, when a source is wrong, or when a reader sends a reproducible fix. Contact: jeromell@be-winner.com (or the contact page).

Advertising

No third-party ads today. If ads are added, privacy, ads.txt, and this page will be updated first.
