Sephiria builds

Official screenshot · Team Horay — A Storm Cloud combo panel in the inventory.

Unofficial fan guide for Sephiria by Team Horay. Community setups for 1.0, not official loadouts.

A Sephiria build is not a wish list — it is a commitment. Pick a combo family, feed it, and spend Tablets on the tags that actually carry the run. The weapon path, the Artifact grid, and the Tablet geometry have to agree, or later floors stall.

What actually makes a build work

  • Force one or two combo families (Storm Cloud, Companion, Ember/Solar, Glacier, Magitech and similar) instead of collecting every tag you see.
  • Use Bond Artifacts to bridge two combos — you usually only need one side active before the bridge pays off.
  • Stack levels into a few carry Artifacts rather than spreading upgrades evenly.
  • Re-place the grid after every new Artifact or Tablet. Tablets reward geometry (lines, columns, crosses), so a good Artifact parked outside an active pattern is dead weight.
  • Pair the run with a costume. Skins change opening stats, so a low-input build and a high-APM build want different ones — see characters and costumes.

Artifact and Tablet pairings

Tablets are the multipliers; Artifacts are the payload. Match Tablet geometry to the family your build already leans on.

Build familyArtifacts to chaseSpend Tablets on
Freeze / Glacier (Dagger), including Blizzard Scythe Frost and Glacier tags, Ice Armament pieces Freeze uptime and elemental damage, not raw attack speed
Companion / summoner Companion-family items (contracts, moles, jellyfish, cushions) Companion count and companion damage

Ranged (Crossbow) Reload and projectile-count pieces Projectile count and pierce

Community build reports also name specific must-have items per route (the freeze route lists an ice-themed trio, for example), but item names move between patches — confirm the item in your own client before rebuilding the whole grid around it. Side Bag storage turns effects off, and a Tablet only pays for the line, column, or cross it actually covers. Those grid rules live on the Artifacts and Tablets page.

Costumes and Talents

Two levers sit outside the grid. Costumes change opening stats, so match the skin to the weapon you plan to push — a low-input freeze route and a high-APM katana do not want the same start. Unlock buckets are on the costume roster.

Talents come from the Destiny Inscription tree after Home Repairs, and they can be respecced. An early defensive or Willpower pick is cheap to undo once the weapon path is clear. Weapon training nodes on that same tree are what unlock branches such as Staff before Morrow’s training ground.

Version notes

Builds are patch-sensitive. The community reports linked below were recorded against 1.0.24-era and other 1.0.x builds, and some authors already marked a route for re-test on a later patch. The shape of a build — one or two combo families, a weapon path, Tablet geometry — ages better than the exact item names.

Before you rebuild a grid around a named piece, check the version in your client and the current notes on Steam or the Steam news feed. Community 1.0 tier notes treat Greatsword as the generous-offer branch and Dagger as the lower-offer, higher-commitment branch, so a route you can finish beats a ceiling you rarely see. Branch notes are on the weapons page. For a floor-3 fight to practice the route against, see Erma.
