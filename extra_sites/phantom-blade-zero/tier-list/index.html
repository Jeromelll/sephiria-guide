Phantom Blade Zero Weapon Tier List

Our Phantom Blade Zero weapon tier list: the ranking method is live now, and the first S-to-C rankings land in launch week from early clears.

🚧 Pre-launch build: Phantom Blade Zero launches October 28, 2026 on PC (Steam) and October 29 on PS5. These pages are structured now and completed during launch week.

There is no honest weapon ranking before October 28 — nobody has finished the game. Here is the method we rank with, and the table that fills in launch week.

How we rank

  1. Boss DPS — damage per opening against the boss roster.
  2. Safety — how forgiving a mistake is mid-combo.
  3. Clear speed — how fast it removes regular enemies.
  4. Access — how early a fresh save can realistically build it.
