Seven Stars sword formation: two-phase fight guide

Version: January 2025 Year of the Snake demonstration. The mechanics below come from S-GAME’s unedited showcase and its January 22 developer explanation. They have not been re-tested here in the October 2026 release. This section does not establish the release-build encounter location, rewards, chapter order or exact attack timings.

The official showcase calls this the Seven Stars formation. S-GAME’s accompanying English press release calls its members the Seven Judgments and names their leader Decree. It describes a coordinated group encounter followed by a suspended-puppet phase.

Phase one: find the blue-robed leader

Six disciples wear white; Decree wears blue and coordinates the formation. Defeating Decree ends this phase. The other disciples sacrifice their health to heal him; if fewer than three disciples remain, Decree spends his health to revive them. S-GAME says the group’s collective health pool stays the same.

Practical approach: look for openings on Decree while watching the disciples around you. A refill of the leader’s bar does not mean all your damage was wasted. The shared-health explanation does not establish a mandatory “kill the healers first” order.

Phase two: wait for the puppet to land

Three disciples move onto platforms and control the leader with red threads. The puppet swings across the arena like a pendulum. S-GAME warns that chasing it recklessly can lead to ineffective attacks. Sufficient damage or depletion of its Sha-Chi makes it touch the ground briefly, creating the stated counterattack opening.

  1. Read the swing before committing. Avoid chasing an airborne target with a long attack string.
  2. Dodge the red-thread ranged attack. The developer says it cannot be blocked.
  3. Answer the powerful thrust with a dodge or parry. It cannot be blocked.
  4. Attack during the brief grounded window after damage or Sha-Chi depletion, then return to reading the next movement.

These response choices describe the demonstration; they do not supply a universal controller binding or a release-build damage threshold. If your current fight differs, use the current game’s cues rather than forcing an older pattern.
