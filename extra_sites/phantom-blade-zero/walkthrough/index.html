Combat fundamentals (confirmed before launch)

  • 30+ main weapons — every main weapon changes your move set, so swapping mid-run is part of the loop.
  • 25 Phantom Edges — secondary weapons carried alongside your main pick.
  • Upgrades and accessories — weapons can be upgraded and paired with accessories.
  • Expansion by victory — the arsenal grows by defeating certain enemies, so boss wins feed back into build options.
