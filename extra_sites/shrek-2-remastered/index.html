Shrek 2 Remastered

Release date, platforms, co-op details and what the remaster changes — all in one page.

Shrek 2 Remastered is Aspyr's modern re-release of the 2004 DreamWorks movie tie-in, one of the most-requested couch co-op games that never made it to current hardware. Aspyr is the studio behind a long line of licensed remasters, and this one keeps the button-mashing co-op adventure format of the original.

November 10, 2026 is the release date displayed on the official Steam store. We have not verified a separate early-access or beta programme.

Co-op and new features

  • Local and online co-op for 2–4 players — the format that made the 2004 game a cult classic.
  • Photo mode — a new addition for the remaster.
  • Improved graphics — a 4K presentation pass over the original art, in the spirit of Aspyr's other remaster work.

Why the remaster matters

The 2004 original never received a modern re-release, so playing it today means tracking down old discs or emulation. The remaster is the first straightforward way to play it on current consoles and PC, and the 2–4 player co-op is the whole point: it is a party game first.
