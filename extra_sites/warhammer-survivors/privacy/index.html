Privacy policy

Last updated October 26, 2026

This policy describes data handling for this unofficial fan guide. The site is published as static pages on Cloudflare Pages. It has no account, sign-in, newsletter, payment, or on-site contact form.

Hosting and request data

When your browser requests a page or asset, Cloudflare may process technical request information such as your IP address, requested URL, browser or device information, and referrer to deliver and protect the site. Cloudflare describes its handling of personal information in its Privacy Policy. We do not control Cloudflare’s infrastructure logs or their retention.

Analytics

No analytics beacon was observed on our homepage when checked on October 26, 2026. Cloudflare Web Analytics, if enabled later, measures page views and site performance without cookies. We will update this policy if our analytics setup changes. See Cloudflare Web Analytics.

Fonts and external links

This site loads fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser contacts Google to request the font stylesheet and font files; Google’s handling of information is described in its Privacy Policy.

Pages may link to third-party websites, including GitHub, Steam, and game-information sources. If you follow a link, that service handles your visit under its own privacy policy.

Corrections submitted through GitHub

The correction link opens a GitHub issue. Issues submitted to the public repository are public, and GitHub processes account and submission information under its Privacy Statement. Do not include sensitive personal information in a public issue.

Advertising and cookies

This site does not currently display Google ads or set Google advertising cookies. Cloudflare or third-party services may process requests or use technologies under their own policies. If advertising is enabled in the future, this policy will be updated and any required consent will be obtained before it is used.

Contact

For privacy questions or corrections, use the contact page.
