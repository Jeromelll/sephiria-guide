Warhammer Survivors Power-Ups & Upgrades

How Warhammer Survivors power-ups work: weapons combine with specific upgrades to evolve, so your picks are a recipe, not a shopping list.

🚧 Demo-era build: Warhammer Survivors launches November 10, 2026. A demo is playable now on Steam and Xbox PC — pages are seeded from demo-era sources and completed at launch.

The core rule

On the Steam page, weapons combine with power-ups and evolve into new forms. In practice that means a run is a recipe: pick a weapon, then hunt the one specific passive it needs — not a scatter of generally good upgrades.

What is confirmed

  • Tempest Axes + Sabaton → Whirlwind Axes (lightning-infused) — confirmed in preview play with Neave Blacktalon.
  • Evolutions need a specific pairing, not a generic “upgrade three times”.
  • Weapon upgrades also add followers: the Lasgun summons Trooper Kozlowski and more Guardsmen join as it upgrades.

How to plan a run

  1. Commit to one weapon early. Spreading upgrades across several weapons leaves you with none evolved.
  2. Learn your target passive before you start. IGN specifically criticised that evolution requirements are unclear in-game — this is the exact problem a recipe list solves.
  3. Watch for summon upgrades. Some power-ups add followers rather than raw damage, which changes the aggro maths.
The demo is the best place to test pairings cheaply — see the demo guide. Full, verified recipes land at the November 10 launch.
