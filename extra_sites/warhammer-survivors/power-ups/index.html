Warhammer Survivors Power-Ups & Upgrades

Warhammer Survivors power-up pairings documented in demo previews, with unconfirmed community reports clearly separated.

Last updated 2026-10-26 · Demo build

🚧 Demo-era evidence: Full power-up names, effects, levels and evolution triggers are not established by the public sources below.

The Steam page says weapons can be mixed with power-ups and evolved into new forms. Only one weapon/passive pairing is fully named in hands-on preview coverage; three more are unconfirmed community reports.

The three community pairings above are leads, not verified rules. Their source is one Steam Community report as maintained by whsurvivors.wiki; the original discussion permalink and independent reproductions are not established here.

Separate weapon progression example

Auroch’s official Squad System post says Kozlowski’s Lasgun upgrades bring in additional Cadian Shocktroopers. That describes weapon progression and followers; it does not establish that a power-up recruits followers.

IGN reports that evolution requirements are unclear in-game. Treat the known pairing as a demo observation, and leave other passives, levels and triggers open until verified in a named build.

Related: pick-ups such as Hellfire Ammo and Ravenak’s Gnashing Jaws apply status effects.
