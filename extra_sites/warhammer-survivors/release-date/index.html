Warhammer Survivors Release Date

Warhammer Survivors is scheduled to launch on November 10, 2026. The October demo is available now on Steam and Xbox PC with six named playable champions.

Last updated 2026-10-24 · Demo build

🚧 Demo-era build: Warhammer Survivors launches November 10, 2026. A demo is playable now on Steam and Xbox PC — pages are seeded from demo-era sources and completed at launch.

Auroch Digital’s release announcement schedules the full game for November 10, 2026 on PC via Steam and GOG, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, Nintendo Switch and Switch 2, iOS and Android. Auroch Digital’s official press release adds that the game will be on Xbox Game Pass at launch. The demo itself is only on Steam and Xbox PC.

Platforms and multiplayer

The developer FAQ, checked October 23, lists Steam, GOG, PS5, Xbox Series X|S, Nintendo Switch 1 and 2, Android and iOS for November 10. It confirms a single-player focus at launch. Game Pass availability at launch comes from the press release; the Xbox PC demo is a separate thing.

In-game followers belong to the squad and keyword system; they are not human co-op partners. For demo access and save-transfer details, see the demo guide.

Store features and ratings

  • Steam: single-player, Steam Achievements, Steam Cloud, Family Sharing, full controller support, keyboard-only option (Steam).
  • Xbox: single player, optimized for Xbox Series X|S, Xbox Play Anywhere, cloud saves (Xbox store).
  • Ratings: ESRB E10+ (Fantasy Violence, Mild Blood); PEGI 7 (Mild Violence) (Steam).
  • Languages: 13 on Steam, including Simplified Chinese — full list in the FAQ.

Is it early access or full launch?

Full launch, not Early Access. The October build is a separate demo. The announced full game has 40+ characters and 75+ weapons; IGN’s preview reports 15+ stages. These are release-scope figures, not the amount available in the demo.

What you can play today

  • Six named demo champions: Malum Caedo, Trooper Kozlowski and the Legend of Catachan in Warhammer 40,000; Neave Blacktalon, Ulgromthier and Lyssa Revenya in Age of Sigmar. Some must be unlocked during play.
  • Stage availability: several maps have been announced, but not every revealed map is confirmed playable in the October demo. Hobby Table access has not been verified here.
  • Enough of the run loop to learn evolutions — see weapon evolutions.

What ships on November 10

  • 40+ characters announced for the full game. The October demo update names six playable champions.
  • 75+ weapons — the demo shows a slice.
  • 15+ stages — including the Hobby Table as a full-game stage.
Earlier-demo saves do not transfer to the October demo. October demo progress also does not transfer to the full game, according to Auroch Digital’s release announcement.
