Warhammer Survivors Release Date

Warhammer Survivors launches November 10, 2026. A playable demo is out now on Steam and Xbox PC — here is exactly what you can play today.

🚧 Demo-era build: Warhammer Survivors launches November 10, 2026. A demo is playable now on Steam and Xbox PC — pages are seeded from demo-era sources and completed at launch.

The November 10 date is stated on the Steam store page and repeated in the official Warhammer Community demo announcement. There has been no announced delay.

Is it early access or full launch?

Full launch. This is not an Early Access release — the October build is a demo, and the game arrives complete on November 10 with 40+ characters, 75+ weapons, and 15+ stages.

What you can play today

  • Two playable heroes: the Legend of Catachan and Lyssa Revenya.
  • Three environments seen in demo coverage: a Tyranid-infested 40K battlefield, a Skaven-themed Age of Sigmar map, and the Hobby Table.
  • Enough of the run loop to learn evolutions — see weapon evolutions.

What ships on November 10

  • 40+ characters — the demo shows two.
  • 75+ weapons — the demo shows a slice.
  • 15+ stages — including the Hobby Table as a full-game stage.
Demo progress does not carry over — treat demo runs as practice and recipe-testing, not progression.
