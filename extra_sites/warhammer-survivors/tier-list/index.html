Warhammer Survivors Tier List

Our Warhammer Survivors character tier list: the ranking method is live now, and the first full S-to-C pass lands with the November 10 launch build.

🚧 Demo-era build: Warhammer Survivors launches November 10, 2026. A demo is playable now on Steam and Xbox PC — pages are seeded from demo-era sources and completed at launch.

With only the demo out, an honest tier list has almost no data. Here is how we will rank, and the table that fills in at launch.

How we rank

  1. Clear speed — how fast a character closes a standard run.
  2. Safety — starting weapon reach and how forgiving mistakes are.
  3. Access — how early in the roster/unlock order the character lands.
