Warhammer Survivors Status Effects

What Burning, Poisoned, Oiled, Stunned, Frenzied, Healing, Berserk and Warded do, where they come from, and the one combo the developer confirms.

Last updated 2026-10-24 · Demo build

🚧 Demo-era build: Warhammer Survivors launches November 10, 2026. A demo is playable now on Steam and Xbox PC — pages are seeded from demo-era sources and completed at launch.

Some weapons, pick-ups and enemies apply temporary status effects on top of damage. Auroch Digital introduced the system in Learn more about the Status Effects system (May 13, 2026) and expanded it in Feature Focus: Status Effects (Aug 5, 2026). The developer calls these “just some” of the effects, so the full list is Pending launch. No durations, damage numbers or percentages have been published, and this page does not invent any.

Oiled + Burning: the confirmed combo

The only combo the developer spells out: douse enemies with Nuln Oil (Oiled), then hit them with a weapon that inflicts Burning. Oiled increases the damage of fire weapons, and Nuln Oil is a cross-universe weapon, so the combo is not limited to one universe’s roster. Nuln Oil is named after the real Citadel shade paint — a hobby joke the developer leans into.

Burning speeds enemies up, Poisoned slows them down

Both deal damage over time and are recommended for chipping down tougher and boss enemies, but they move enemies in opposite directions: burning enemies get faster, poisoned ones slower. Oiled also slows its targets, which takes some sting out of Burning’s speed-up.

Effects that hit your champion

  • Poisoned: the Death Guard poison the player through auras and weapon attacks; Myphitic Blight-hauler missiles and Miasmic Malignifier fumes apply it (June 10 post); Skaven Acolyte Globadiers leave poison clouds (Sept 3 post).
  • Berserk (buff): the Frenzon pick-up increases how often your weapons attack.
  • Healing (buff): regenerates lost health.
  • Some enemies are immune to status effects; which ones is Pending launch.

Status effects inside faction traits

  • Tyranid Synapse: critical bioforms such as the Hive Tyrant give nearby Termagants and Hormagaunts Berserk; kill the Synapse creature and the lesser bioforms fall into a temporary stunned shock (Faction Traits, Sept 17).
  • Ork Waaagh!: when the screen starts turning red, more Orks spawn and enemies become frenzied.
  • Skaven Bell of Doom: grants nearby Skaven Warded.

Practical takeaways

  • Prioritise Synapse creatures in Tyranid stages — removing them buffs you twice (no more Berserk, plus a stun).
  • Expect a burning crowd to close in faster; kite before you ignite, or Oil first.
  • Keep moving out of green fog and poison clouds; Poisoned stacks up damage over time.
  • Status effects pair with evolutions — check weapon evolutions and squad followers for weapons you can bring.
