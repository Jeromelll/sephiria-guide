Answers

When does Warhammer Survivors come out?

It came out on 10 November 2026 on every platform. The unlock hour was published on 9 November: 10am GMT, 11am CET, 2am PST and 5am EST (release information post). Platform list per the official FAQ. Patch 2.1.8 is the current build on Steam (25 November 2026).

Which platforms is it on?

Steam, GOG, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, Nintendo Switch 1 and 2, and mobile (Google Play and iOS) (official FAQ). The official press release also says it will be on Xbox Game Pass at launch — it shipped on Game Pass on 10 November 2026. On Steam it is Windows-only; Mac and Linux are not listed (Steam store page).

How much does it cost?

Steam lists $7.99 in the US and £6.39 in the UK (Steam price API, checked 27 November 2026). The announced price was £6.39 | $7.99 | €7.49 with a 10% launch discount (9 November post); the discount window has closed and the store now shows the full price. Regional prices follow the storefront — see release date & price.

Does it have Chinese language support?

Yes for Simplified Chinese: it is in the Steam store page’s supported-language list (English, French, Italian, German, Spanish – Spain, Japanese, Korean, Polish, Portuguese – Brazil, Russian, Simplified Chinese, Turkish, Ukrainian). Traditional Chinese is not listed. Steam does not mark any language with full audio. Source: Steam store page, checked 27 November 2026.

Can I play with a controller?

Yes. Steam lists Full Controller Support, including DualShock and DualSense controllers, plus a keyboard-only option (Steam store page).

Is it Steam Deck Verified?

Yes. Auroch Digital announced Steam Deck Verified status on October 16, 2026 (Steam post); Valve’s compatibility report lists default controls, on-screen glyphs, legible text and default performance as passing. External controllers are not supported as the primary player on Deck (Steam Deck compatibility report).

Is there co-op or online multiplayer?

No. There is no co-op or competitive multiplayer as of 27 November 2026. The official FAQ says the team is focusing on the single-player experience and will tell the community if multiplayer is added later. In-game squad followers are AI allies, not other players — see the squad system guide.

Is there a demo, and does my progress carry over?

A free demo is on Steam and Xbox PC (press release). Its October 6 build adds the Legend of Catachan and Lyssa Revenya. Progress from the earlier demo does not carry over to this one, and demo progress does not transfer to the full game (release announcement). Details: demo guide.

Is it on Xbox Game Pass?

Yes — Auroch Digital’s press release of October 6, 2026 said the game would be on Xbox Game Pass at launch. The demo is a separate Xbox PC build.

Can I pre-order it?

Pre-ordering is over: the game released on 10 November 2026. Pre-orders ran on PlayStation 5 from October 6, Nintendo Switch 1 and 2 from October 6 in select territories, and Xbox from October 27, 2026, with a discount on select platforms (release announcement). No Steam pre-purchase package was ever listed.

Is it a Vampire Survivors DLC?

No. It is a standalone game built on the foundation of Vampire Survivors with poncle, but every character, stage, enemy, power-up and secret was made from scratch (official FAQ).

Which enemy factions are in the game?

Gloomspite Gitz, Skaven, Blades of Khorne, Tyranids, Orks and the Death Guard (official FAQ). Short explanations are in the glossary.

Can 40K and Age of Sigmar characters fight together?

No. Each universe has its own roster, weapons, enemies, stages and progression, with some shared weapons (Nov 11, 2025 FAQ post). The October demo adds a slight cross-universe effect to meta-progression (Oct 19 post).

What PC do I need?

Minimum on Steam: 64-bit Windows 10, dual-core 2.4 GHz x86-64 CPU, 4 GB RAM, GTX 750 or equivalent, 5 GB storage. Steam lists no recommended tier beyond “requires a 64-bit processor and operating system” (checked 27 November 2026). There is no Mac or Linux build. Full table on the release date page.
