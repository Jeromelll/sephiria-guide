Warhammer Survivors Demo Guide

The October 2026 Warhammer Survivors demo is available on Steam and Xbox PC. Auroch Digital names six playable champions; some must be unlocked during play.

Last updated 2026-10-24 · Demo build

🚧 Demo-era build: Warhammer Survivors launches November 10, 2026. A demo is playable now on Steam and Xbox PC — pages are seeded from demo-era sources and completed at launch.

Where to get it

  • Steam — the demo is listed on the store page (app 3669620).
  • Xbox PC — the official announcement names Xbox PC alongside Steam.

The updated demo launched on October 6, 2026. This guide uses the roster described in Auroch Digital’s October 19 demo update.

The demo features Malum Caedo, Trooper Kozlowski and the Legend of Catachan in Warhammer 40,000; Neave Blacktalon, Ulgromthier and Lyssa Revenya in Age of Sigmar. Catachan and Lyssa are the two newly introduced champions, not the entire demo roster. The full game’s announced 40-plus roster is separate.

What to actually test in the demo

  1. One evolution, end to end. Play Neave Blacktalon, take Tempest Axes, and hunt the Sabaton passive — that teaches you the recipe shape better than any list.
  2. Champion and follower roles. Trooper Kozlowski is a playable champion who starts with a Lasgun. He can also appear as a follower in another champion’s squad. Watch how weapon Keywords affect who can use a weapon.
  3. Difficulty curve. Every preview flagged a harsh opening; use demo runs to find your comfort pace before launch.

What the press said about this demo

  • PC Gamer (10 hours): praised heroes, themed maps, recruitable followers, bosses; flagged intense early difficulty; Steam Deck performance good.
  • IGN: a celebration of both Warhammer settings rather than a clone; criticised slow repeat openings and unclear evolution requirements.
  • G2A News: praised progression, pixel art, companion-summoning weapons; found character-rescue objectives frustrating.
The full game is scheduled for November 10, 2026. See the release date and platform guide. Earlier-demo saves do not transfer to the October demo, and October demo progress does not transfer to the full game.
