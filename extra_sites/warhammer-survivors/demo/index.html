Warhammer Survivors Demo Guide

The Warhammer Survivors demo is out now on Steam and Xbox PC with two playable heroes and three stages. Here is what is in it and what to test.

🚧 Demo-era build: Warhammer Survivors launches November 10, 2026. A demo is playable now on Steam and Xbox PC — pages are seeded from demo-era sources and completed at launch.

Where to get it

  • Steam — the demo is listed on the store page (app 3669620).
  • Xbox PC — the official announcement names Xbox PC alongside Steam.

The current build went live October 6, 2026 and incorporates community feedback from the earlier demo.

What is in it

ContentDemo
Playable heroesThe Legend of Catachan · Lyssa Revenya
StagesTyranid 40K battlefield · Skaven Age of Sigmar map · Hobby Table
WeaponsA slice of the 75+ arsenal, including the Lasgun, Chainsword, Boltgun and Nuln Oil
EvolutionsAvailable — Tempest Axes + Sabaton was confirmed in preview play

What to actually test in the demo

  1. One evolution, end to end. Play Neave Blacktalon, take Tempest Axes, and hunt the Sabaton passive — that teaches you the recipe shape better than any list.
  2. The Lasgun’s summon. It calls in Trooper Kozlowski, and more Guardsmen join as it upgrades — a second progression axis to watch.
  3. Difficulty curve. Every preview flagged a harsh opening; use demo runs to find your comfort pace before launch.

What the press said about this demo

  • PC Gamer (10 hours): praised heroes, themed maps, recruitable followers, bosses; flagged intense early difficulty; Steam Deck performance good.
  • IGN: a celebration of both Warhammer settings rather than a clone; criticised slow repeat openings and unclear evolution requirements.
  • G2A News: praised progression, pixel art, companion-summoning weapons; found character-rescue objectives frustrating.
