Warhammer Survivors Beginner Guide

Start Warhammer Survivors right: what the current demo teaches, how paint pots work, and the run habits that carry into the November 10 launch.

Last updated 2026-10-24 · Demo build

🚧 Demo-era build: Warhammer Survivors launches November 10, 2026. A demo is playable now on Steam and Xbox PC — pages are seeded from demo-era sources and completed at launch.

Play the demo now

The October demo is available on Steam and Xbox PC. Auroch Digital’s October 19 update names six playable champions: Malum Caedo, Trooper Kozlowski and the Legend of Catachan in Warhammer 40,000; Neave Blacktalon, Ulgromthier and Lyssa Revenya in Age of Sigmar. Some require in-game unlocks. Start with the demo guide; the character comparison page does not yet contain a tested ranking.

Paint pots

Paint pots are the game’s collectible: they unlock character forms and secrets. Collecting them is part of the long-term progression, not a side detour — treat any pot route you learn in the demo as launch-relevant knowledge.

Survivors-genre basics that apply here

As in the whole genre: keep moving, let auto-fire do the aiming, and choose upgrades that stack with your weapon instead of against it. Warhammer Survivors adds its own layer on top — weapons combine with power-ups and evolve into new forms — so plan your combo before it locks in. See weapon evolutions.

Know what you are walking into

Every hands-on preview flagged the same thing: the early difficulty is harsh (PC Gamer, after 10 hours) and openings can feel slow (IGN). Rescue objectives, which require pushing into dangerous ground, were called frustrating by G2A. Budget a few failed runs before the loop clicks — that is the intended shape, not you playing badly.
