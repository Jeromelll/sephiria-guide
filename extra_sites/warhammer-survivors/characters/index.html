How the squad and keyword system works

Auroch Digital’s October 21 Squad System announcement explains that characters, weapons and some items have keywords. When your champion acquires a weapon their keywords cannot use, an eligible ally joins and wields it for them. Multiple followers can accompany one champion. Full breakdown: squad system guide.

  • Followers cannot take damage or collect items. They extend your attacks; the player still handles collection.
  • Bastian Carthalos + Lorai: Lorai’s Nebulous Sea Fog pulls enemies into Bastian’s close-range Uskavar attacks.
  • The Castellan + Trooper Kozlowski: Lasgun upgrades bring additional Cadian Shocktroopers, supporting the Castellan’s follower-based damage scaling.

These are developer-described examples, not a tested tier ranking or weapon-evolution recipe. The official FAQ confirms a single-player focus at launch; followers do not establish online or local co-op support. Compare the separate evolution requirements when planning a loadout.

Playable champions and squad followers

Trooper Kozlowski is a playable Warhammer 40,000 champion who starts with a Lasgun. He can also join another champion’s squad as a follower. Playable champions and followers are not mutually exclusive character categories.

As explained in the October 21 Squad System update, weapon Keywords affect whether a champion can wield a weapon directly. An eligible ally can use a weapon for them instead; followers attack but do not take damage or collect items.

Announced beyond the demo

Sebastian Yarrick is named on the official Steam page. He is not included in the six-character demo roster listed above. The complete launch roster and individual unlock requirements remain to be checked in the release build.

Character unlocks

Character-rescue objectives and paint-pot collectibles are described in demo coverage. We have not reproduced every unlock route, so this guide does not assign exact thresholds or a universal unlock method to all six champions.
