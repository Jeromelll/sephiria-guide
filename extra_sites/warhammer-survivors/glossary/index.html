Warhammer Survivors Glossary

Factions, units and game terms from Warhammer 40,000 and Age of Sigmar, each explained in one line with official and lore links.

Last updated 2026-10-24 · Demo build

🚧 Demo-era build: Warhammer Survivors launches November 10, 2026. A demo is playable now on Steam and Xbox PC — pages are seeded from demo-era sources and completed at launch.

New to Warhammer? These are the factions, units and nouns that appear in Warhammer Survivors and its official announcements, explained in one line each. “Official post” links show where the term appears in the game’s own news; Lexicanum (a fan-run Warhammer wiki) has the deeper lore. Official Warhammer news and setting articles live on Warhammer Community.

Game terms

TermMeaningLinks
KeywordsTags on every character, weapon and some items that decide who can wield what. Off-Keyword weapons bring a follower.Guide · Official post
FollowerAn ally who wields a weapon for your champion. Cannot take damage or collect items.Guide · Official post
Status effectTemporary effects such as Burning, Poisoned and Oiled.Guide · Official post
Faction TraitsTabletop-inspired mechanics unique to each enemy faction, e.g. Synapse and Waaagh!.Official post
Extremis BossEach enemy faction’s signature boss, such as Ghazghkull or the Great Unclean One.Official post
EvolutionCombining a weapon with the right power-up to transform it — sometimes summoning a hero.Guide · Official post
Pick-upTemporary items found around faction-themed light sources, mostly unique to each universe.Guide · Official post
Cross-universe weaponA weapon usable in both universes, such as the D6 Dice and Nuln Oil.Official post
Nuln OilA real Citadel shade paint; in-game a cross-universe weapon that applies Oiled.Guide · Official post
Paint potsCollectibles on the battlefield that unlock new character forms and secrets.Steam
Hobby TableA stage set on a hobbyist’s desk where several enemy factions attack at once.Guide · Official post

Warhammer 40,000

TermMeaningLinks
ImperiumThe vast human empire ruled in the name of the God-Emperor; every 40K champion in the demo fights for it.Lexicanum · Steam page
Space Marines (Adeptus Astartes)Genetically enhanced super-soldiers in power armour. Malum Caedo and Brother Luca are Space Marines.Lexicanum · Official post
UltramarinesThe Space Marine Chapter of Malum Caedo, Brother Luca and Chapter Master Marneus Calgar.Lexicanum · Official post
Sternguard VeteranA veteran Space Marine skilled with many weapons — Malum Caedo’s rank.Lexicanum · Official post
IntercessorA front-line Space Marine armed with a bolt rifle; Brother Luca is one, starting with a Boltgun.Lexicanum · Official post
Black TemplarsA crusading Space Marine Chapter. Their Castellan grows stronger with every follower gained.Lexicanum · Official post
Astra MilitarumThe Imperial Guard: the Imperium’s mass army of ordinary human soldiers.Lexicanum · Official post
Cadian Shock TroopsDisciplined Astra Militarum regiments from Cadia. Trooper Kozlowski is one; his Lasgun brings more Shocktroopers as it upgrades.Lexicanum · Official post
CatachanA jungle death-world whose regiments excel at guerrilla warfare — home of the Legend of Catachan.Lexicanum · Official post
CommissarAn Imperial officer who enforces discipline. Commissar Sebastian Yarrick is named as playable on the Steam page.Lexicanum · Steam page
Leman RussThe Imperial Guard’s main battle tank, commanded in-game by Knight-Commander Pask.Lexicanum · Official post
TyranidsAn alien swarm that devours whole planets for biomass. A 40K enemy faction.Lexicanum · Official post
Hive Mind / SynapseThe Tyranid collective will. In-game, Synapse creatures such as the Hive Tyrant drive nearby swarms Berserk; kill them to stun the swarm.Lexicanum · Official post
OrksBrutal, war-loving green-skinned aliens. Led in-game by Ghazghkull Mag Uruk Thraka, an Extremis Boss.Lexicanum · Official post
Waaagh!An Ork war-surge. In-game the screen turns red, more Orks spawn and they become frenzied.Lexicanum · Official post
Death GuardChaos Space Marines devoted to Nurgle, god of decay. A 40K enemy faction that poisons the player.Lexicanum · Official post
NurgleThe Chaos god of plague, decay and despair, worshipped by the Death Guard.Lexicanum · Official post
Space HulkA drifting mass of fused, derelict starships. The Gallowdark stage is one.Lexicanum · Official post
RecaffThe Imperium’s caffeinated drink, a running joke in the developer’s posts.Lexicanum · Official post

Age of Sigmar

TermMeaningLinks
Mortal RealmsThe eight magical realms where Age of Sigmar takes place.Lexicanum · Steam page
SigmarThe God-King who leads the forces of Order.Lexicanum · Official post
Stormcast EternalsSigmar’s lightning-forged immortal warriors. Neave Blacktalon and Sharynn Azurwrath are Stormcast.Lexicanum · Official post
RealmgateA magical portal between realms. Hammerhal’s Stormrift Realmgate lets you switch biomes mid-stage.Lexicanum · Official post
HammerhalThe grandest of the Free Cities, spanning Aqshy and Ghyran; an in-game stage besieged by Skaven and Blades of Khorne.Lexicanum · Official post
Aqshy / Ghyran / ShyishThe Realms of Fire, Life and Death. The Steam page mentions battles on the ashen plains of Aqshy; Shyish gives the Purple Sun pick-up its name.Lexicanum · Official post
FyreslayersDuardin (dwarf) warrior-cults hunting ur-gold. Ulgromthier is an Auric Runemaster; the Fyrestorm Greataxe is a Fyreslayer weapon.Lexicanum · Official post
DuardinThe dwarf peoples of the Mortal Realms.Lexicanum · Official post
Gotrek GurnissonThe legendary Slayer. Playable, and summoned by evolving the Fyrestorm Greataxe into Zangrom-Thaz.Lexicanum · Official post
SkavenScheming rat-men who serve the Great Horned Rat. An Age of Sigmar enemy faction.Lexicanum · Official post
WarpstoneRaw, corrupting magical rock that the Skaven hoard and build weapons from.Lexicanum · Official post
Rat OgorA hulking Skaven brute; in-game it unleashes Warp Fury at low health.Lexicanum · Official post
Blades of KhorneDaemons and mortals devoted to Khorne, the Blood God. Includes Flesh Hounds, Bloodcrushers and Skull Cannons.Lexicanum · Official post
Gloomspite GitzMoon-worshipping grots with Squigs and Troggoths. Revealed as an enemy faction on October 6.Lexicanum · Official post
SquigBouncing, mostly-mouth creatures bred by grots; “ravenous Squigs” back the Gloomspite Gitz.Lexicanum · Official post
TroggothHuge, regenerating, dim-witted brutes that fight alongside the Gloomspite Gitz.Lexicanum · Official post
Endless SpellA summoned spell that persists on the battlefield. In-game, Endless Spells are AoS pick-ups such as the Purple Sun of Shyish.Lexicanum · Official post
