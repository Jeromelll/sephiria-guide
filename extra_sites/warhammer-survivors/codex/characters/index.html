Squad & keyword system

From Auroch Digital’s October 21 Feature Focus: every character, weapon and some items carry one or more keywords. When your champion acquires a weapon their keywords do not allow, an ally who can use it joins as a follower. Followers attack on your behalf but cannot take damage or collect items, and several can squad up with one champion. Some champions are restricted to signature weapons (Marneus Calgar’s Gauntlets of Ultramar, Gotrek’s Zangrom-Thaz).

The full launch roster is 40+ characters (Steam page); the 18 below are the ones named in official or preview sources so far. Individual keyword lists are pending launch.
