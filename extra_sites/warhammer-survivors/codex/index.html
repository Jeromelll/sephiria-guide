Codex FAQ

What does the Warhammer Survivors codex cover?

Three reference lists: 18 characters with their weapons, abilities and unlocks; 47 weapons, evolutions, passives and pick-ups; and 39 enemies across 7 groups, including elites and Extremis Bosses. Each card links to the section it belongs to.

What do the official, press and community labels mean?

Official is developer, publisher or store text. Press is hands-on preview reporting. Community is player reports that have not been verified against an official source. The label is shown on every entry so you can weigh it before acting on it.

Is any of this datamined or guessed?

No. Every entry cites where it came from, and anything not published yet is left marked Not verified rather than filled in. The Sources list at the bottom names each outlet that was checked.

How current is the codex?

Check the date at the top of this page: it states the build the index was last checked against. Values can change with a patch, so re-check a number in the client before you spend a run on it.
