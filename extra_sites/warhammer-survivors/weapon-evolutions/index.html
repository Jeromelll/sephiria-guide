Warhammer Survivors Weapon Evolutions

How Warhammer Survivors weapon evolutions work, the 75+ weapon list beginnings, and the community-reported evolution recipes to verify at launch.

🚧 Demo-era build: Warhammer Survivors launches November 10, 2026. A demo is playable now on Steam and Xbox PC — pages are seeded from demo-era sources and completed at launch.

Evolution is the build-defining mechanic: on the Steam page, weapons can be combined with power-ups and evolved into new forms — the survivors-like equivalent of a build’s final piece.

Weapons confirmed so far

The Steam page names a 75+ weapon arsenal, including:

  • Boltgun
  • Astartes Chainsword
  • Whirlwind Axes
  • Nuln Oil
