Weapons that summon companions

The keyword and squad system explains who wields an incompatible weapon. Recruiting a follower does not by itself verify an evolution pairing or its level requirements.

Preview coverage reports that the Lasgun can bring Trooper Kozlowski into another champion’s squad, with more Guardsmen joining as it upgrades — separate from evolution.

The published sources do not establish a complete recipe list, required levels, or general evolution trigger. Treat the community cards as leads until each is reproduced in a named build.
