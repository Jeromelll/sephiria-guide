Warhammer Survivors Weapon Evolutions

How Warhammer Survivors weapon evolutions work, the 75+ weapon list beginnings, and the community-reported evolution recipes to verify at launch.

🚧 Demo-era build: Warhammer Survivors launches November 10, 2026. A demo is playable now on Steam and Xbox PC — pages are seeded from demo-era sources and completed at launch.

Evolution is the build-defining mechanic: on the Steam page, weapons can be combined with power-ups and evolved into new forms — the survivors-like equivalent of a build’s final piece.

Weapons confirmed so far

The Steam page names a 75+ weapon arsenal, including:

  • BBoltgun
  • AAstartes Chainsword
  • WWhirlwind Axes
  • NNuln Oil

Confirmed recipes

One recipe has been verified in preview coverage rather than community guesswork. IGN’s October 6 preview played Neave Blacktalon and confirmed her starter weapon evolves on a specific passive:

TWeaponTempest Axes
+
SPassive requiredSabaton
→
WEvolves intoWhirlwind Axes (lightning-infused)

✅ Confirmed by IGN preview

This is the shape of the whole system: a weapon plus one specific passive, not a generic “upgrade three times.” IGN specifically criticised that evolution requirements are unclear in-game — which is exactly why recipes get searched for.

Weapons that summon companions

Some weapons are also recruiters. The Lasgun calls in Trooper Bartosz Kozlowski, and further Guardsmen join as the weapon upgrades — a second progression axis on top of evolution.

Launch week (November 10) replaces the unconfirmed table with recipes verified in the release build — pairing requirements, level thresholds, and the full evolved-weapon list.
