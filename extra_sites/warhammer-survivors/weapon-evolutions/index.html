Warhammer Survivors Weapon Evolutions

Every Warhammer Survivors weapon evolution reported so far in one list: the IGN-confirmed recipe, all community-reported weapon + power-up combos, and how evolution works.

Last updated 2026-11-02 · Demo build

🚧 Demo-era build: Warhammer Survivors launches November 10, 2026. A demo is playable now on Steam and Xbox PC — pages are seeded from demo-era sources and completed at launch.
This page holds the full list: the illustrated cards for the confirmed recipe, then the complete demo pairing table with the source of every row labelled. Community/datamine rows are leads, not patch notes — no damage numbers are listed.

Evolution is the build-defining mechanic: on the Steam page, weapons can be combined with power-ups and evolved into new forms — the survivors-like equivalent of a build’s final piece. IGN specifically criticised that evolution requirements are unclear in-game — which is exactly why the recipe list gets searched for.

All reported recipes (demo build)

Count them as eleven unique recipes. Nuln Oil + Hourglass = Mordant Earth is reported for both Warhammer 40,000 and Age of Sigmar, which is why some tables show twelve rows. The October community post does not give levels. CapyDB’s datamine says each base weapon maxes at 8 and that the passive only has to be in the inventory. Treat that sentence as a datamine. Official weapon facts that are not the pairing

  • Chainsword — Malum Caedo’s starting weapon, horizontal serrated attacks. The Eviscerator result is not in that post.
  • Lasgun — fires at the nearest enemy; levelling it adds Cadian squadmates; taking it on another champion recruits Trooper Kozlowski. Meltagun is the reported evolution, not the official description of those levels.
  • Boltgun — Brother Luca’s starter. The developer says it evolves into a Heavy Bolter with a stream of fire. Dice Cube is the reported companion.
  • Whirlwind Battery — missiles from above in the IGN hands-on. The developer names Orbital Bombardment as the evolution. Liquid Green Stuff is the October reported companion; a May community list said Tape Measure.
  • Nuln Oil — a cross-universe weapon that applies Oiled, so fire damage hurts more. Mordant Earth is the reported evolution. See status effects.
  • Rope and Grapnel — hooks from the screen edge. The developer describes the Ghost of Rhea evolution. White Dwarf is only in the community / datamine column.

Weapons that summon companions

The keyword and squad system explains who wields an incompatible weapon. Recruiting a follower does not by itself verify an evolution pairing or its level requirements.

Some weapons are also recruiters. The Lasgun calls in Trooper Bartosz Kozlowski, and further Guardsmen join as the weapon upgrades — a second progression axis on top of evolution.

Launch week (November 10) replaces the unconfirmed table with recipes verified in the release build — pairing requirements, level thresholds, and the full evolved-weapon list.
