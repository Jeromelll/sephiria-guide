About this site

An unofficial fan guide · Not affiliated with Games Workshop, Auroch Digital, or the game’s publisher

This independent fan guide organizes information about Warhammer Survivors, including its characters, weapons, stages, and demo. It is not an official game site and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Games Workshop, Auroch Digital, or the game’s publisher.

Guide pages are assembled from publicly available material and are updated as information changes. If you spot an error or have a correction, please use the GitHub issue form.
