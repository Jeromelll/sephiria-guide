Stages seen in the demo

  • Tyranid-infested 40K battlefield — the 40,000 setting.
  • Skaven-themed Age of Sigmar map — with Skaven attack formations.
  • The Hobby Table — a full-game stage built from paint pots, tools, and mixed factions.

What the press says

  • PC Gamer (10 hours with the demo): praised heroes, themed maps, recruitable followers and boss fights; flagged intense early difficulty. Steam Deck performance reported good.
  • IGN: called it a celebration of both settings rather than a straight clone; criticised slow repeat openings and unclear evolution requirements.
  • G2A News: praised progression, pixel art and companion-summoning weapons; found character-rescue objectives frustrating.

What the demo has

The October 6 demo includes the Legend of Catachan and Lyssa Revenya as playable characters — enough to learn the run loop and test early evolution recipes.
