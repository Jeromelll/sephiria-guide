Warhammer Survivors: What It Is, Platforms, Price Status & Release Date

Warhammer Survivors is a standalone survivors-like by Auroch Digital, made with poncle. It launches on 10 November 2026. The Steam price is still TBD. Steam is the first storefront, with GOG, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, Nintendo Switch, Switch 2, iOS, Android and Xbox Game Pass also announced.

🚧 Demo-era build: Warhammer Survivors launches November 10, 2026. A demo is playable now on Steam and Xbox PC — pages are seeded from demo-era sources and completed at launch.

Warhammer Survivors is its own game, not a Vampire Survivors downloadable add-on. Auroch Digital’s official FAQ says it is built on the foundation of Vampire Survivors with poncle, and that every character, stage, enemy, power-up and secret was made from scratch. The 4 November 2025 announcement calls it a roguelike survivors game in which you choose a character, collect and evolve wargear, and destroy endless swarms. That is the product: two Warhammer settings, auto-firing weapons, and runs that end when the horde does.

What the game is

A run is the whole session. You pick a champion, move through a stage, and weapons fire without a manual aim button. Experience from defeated enemies is spent on weapons and passives. Some weapons combine with a specific passive and evolve into a new form. That evolution step is the survivors-like “final piece” of a build, and it is the part players search for by name. How that step works, and the October demo pairings, are on the evolutions hub. One pairing was also played in an IGN preview. The rest of the demo recipe table is a community report and a file datamine, not an official list, and this site does not print damage numbers for any of them.

Paint pots are the announced collectible. They unlock character forms and secrets. They are part of long-term progression, not a separate mode. The Steam page also describes stages with their own enemy factions and secrets, including the open ashen plains of Aqshy and the corridors of an abandoned Space Hulk.

The game is single-player at launch. The official FAQ says the team is focusing on the single-player experience and will tell the community if multiplayer is added later. In-game followers are AI allies recruited by weapons and Keywords. They are not other players. Steam lists the store feature as single-player. Xbox lists single player as well.

Price status: TBD

The price status is TBD. When the Steam page was checked on 24 October 2026 it did not show a price, and the Xbox store page did not show one either. Until a storefront displays a number, that is the purchase price: not yet shown.

Separately, Auroch Digital’s FAQ has published £6.39 | $7.99 | €7.49, and says the figure may vary slightly by platform and region. That is an announced figure, not the price printed on Steam. It is not a sale price. If Steam later shows a different number, the Steam page wins for Steam.

Release date

The full release is 10 November 2026 on every announced platform. Auroch Digital has published the date and has not published an unlock hour, so the clock time in the UK, the United States, Europe and China is confirm at launch / TBD. It is a full launch, not Early Access. The October build is a separate demo.

The updated demo went live on 6 October 2026. An earlier demo had already been played by a large audience: the July community update said more than 190,000 players tried the first demo and more than half played over an hour. That first demo left the Steam store and remained playable until 22 July 2026. Saves from the earlier demo do not carry into the October demo, and October demo progress does not transfer to the full game. Both rules are in the release announcement. What a retail save file looks like on disk, and whether a cloud conflict can be resolved in a menu, has not been described. Steam lists Steam Cloud. Xbox lists cloud saves. Those are store badges, not a published save-system manual.

A developer Reddit AMA on r/Games was scheduled for 27 October 2026, 6–8pm GMT, in the Squad System Steam post. This page does not treat that AMA as a source until a transcript is published.

Announced scope

The full game is advertised with 40+ characters and 75+ weapons. IGN’s 6 October 2026 preview reports 15+ stages. That stage count is a press figure, not a line in the official FAQ, so it stays labelled as IGN’s number until the release build lists the maps. The October demo is a slice of that scope, not the whole list.

Auroch Digital’s 19 October update names six playable demo champions. Warhammer 40,000: Malum Caedo, Trooper Kozlowski and the Legend of Catachan. Age of Sigmar: Neave Blacktalon, Ulgromthier and Lyssa Revenya. Catachan and Lyssa are the two champions introduced with that October demo. Some of the six must be unlocked during play, so a fresh demo save does not necessarily start with all six selectable. Sebastian Yarrick is named on the Steam page and is not part of that six. The rest of the 40+ roster, including who is playable on day one versus who is an unlock, is confirm at launch / TBD. Named champions and followers are tracked on the characters page.

Enemy factions named in the official FAQ are the Gloomspite Gitz, Skaven, Blades of Khorne, Tyranids, Orks and the Death Guard. Stages named in announcements include Imperial Bastion and The Great Parch (the original demo), plus Hammerhal, Gallowdark and the Hobby Table (21 May 2026 Steam post). A revealed map is not automatically in the October demo. The Hobby Table, described in the 17 June post, exists in both universes and can mix several factions from that universe. Whether it is playable in the current demo has not been verified here. Stage notes stay on the stages page.

Two settings that do not mix in a run

Warhammer 40,000 and Age of Sigmar each have their own roster, weapons, enemies, stages and progression. The 11 November 2025 FAQ post says some weapons are shared. Nuln Oil and the D6 Dice are the cross-universe weapons described in later posts. Characters from the two settings do not fight in the same run. The October demo adds a slight cross-universe effect to meta-progression, which is account-level rather than a shared battlefield. How large that shared meta-progression is — a currency, a counter, or something else — is only described as “slight” in the 19 October post, so the exact rule is confirm at launch / TBD.

Squads are allies, not co-op

The 21 October Squad System post explains Keywords. Every character and every weapon has one or more Keywords, and so do some items. If your champion takes a weapon their Keywords do not allow, an ally who can use it joins and wields it. That ally is a follower. Followers attack. They cannot take damage and they cannot collect items. Several followers can accompany one champion. The exact cap is confirm at launch / TBD.

Two weapons are also recruiters in the official posts, separate from a generic off-Keyword pickup. Trooper Kozlowski’s Lasgun fires at the nearest enemy and, as it levels, brings Cadian squadmates. Taking the Lasgun on another champion recruits Kozlowski. Evolving the Fyrestorm Greataxe calls Gotrek Gurnisson with Zangrom-Thaz. Evolving the Power Fists calls Marneus Calgar with the Gauntlets of Ultramar. Those two evolutions have their own pages because the result is official and the required passive is not. The squad write-up, citing those posts, also says that playing as the summoned hero does not spawn a clone.

None of that is online or local co-op. If a co-op mode is added after launch, the official FAQ says the team will say so. Until that post exists, co-op is not a launch feature.

How weapons evolve

On the Steam page, weapons can be combined with power-ups and evolved into new forms. IGN’s preview of Neave Blacktalon is the only pairing a press outlet has walked through in public: Tempest Axes plus the Sabaton passive become the lightning-infused Whirlwind Axes. IGN also said the in-game requirement is unclear. The October demo’s wider pairing table — eleven unique recipes, including Chainsword, Lasgun and Nuln Oil — comes from a community report and from datamines that read demo files. Those rows are not developer patch notes. The passive for Gotrek’s axe and for Calgar’s gauntlets has not been published. No damage numbers are printed here.

PC, Deck, controller and cloud

Steam’s minimum specification, checked 24 October 2026, is 64-bit Windows 10, a dual-core 2.4 GHz x86-64 processor, 4 GB of RAM, a GeForce GTX 750 or equivalent, and 5 GB of storage. Recommended specifications are confirm at launch / TBD. There is no macOS or Linux depot on that page. The same table is kept on the release-date page so this page does not become a second requirements manual.

Steam lists full controller support, including DualShock and DualSense, and a keyboard-only option. Auroch Digital announced Steam Deck Verified status on 16 October 2026. Valve’s compatibility report lists default controls, on-screen glyphs, legible text and default performance as passing. External controllers are not supported as the primary player on Deck. Store features on Steam also include achievements, Steam Cloud and Family Sharing. Xbox lists cloud saves and Xbox Play Anywhere. Whether Family Sharing, Play Anywhere or Steam Cloud covers demo progress is not stated; the demo-transfer rule above is the only save rule the developer has spelled out.

Languages and ratings

Steam’s supported-language list, checked 24 October 2026, is English, French, Italian, German, Spanish (Spain), Japanese, Korean, Polish, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Simplified Chinese, Turkish and Ukrainian. Traditional Chinese is not on that list. Steam does not mark any of those languages with full audio. Age ratings on the Steam page are ESRB E10+ (Fantasy Violence, Mild Blood) and PEGI 7 (Mild Violence).

What preview coverage actually said

PC Gamer, after about ten hours in the demo, praised the heroes, themed maps, recruitable followers and boss fights, and flagged intense early difficulty. Steam Deck performance in that piece was described as good. IGN called the game a celebration of both Warhammer settings rather than a straight clone, and criticised slow repeat openings and unclear evolution requirements. G2A News praised progression, the pixel art and companion-summoning weapons, and found character-rescue objectives frustrating. Those are impressions of a demo, not patch notes and not a ranking. A launch-week ordering of champions is not on this page.
