Warhammer Survivors Evolution List

The list of evolution results named so far is eleven unique October demo pairings from community and datamine sources, plus developer-named forms whose companion item has not been published.

Last updated 2026-10-27 · Demo build

🚧 Demo-era build: Warhammer Survivors launches November 10, 2026. A demo is playable now on Steam and Xbox PC — pages are seeded from demo-era sources and completed at launch.

Names that are easy to mix up

  • Whirlwind Axes are the evolution of Tempest Axes. Whirlwind Battery is a different weapon and evolves, in the reports, into Orbital Bombardment.
  • Dice Cube is the reported passive for Boltgun and for Boltstorm Pistol. D6 Dice is a separate cross-universe weapon in the developer posts. They are not documented as the same item.
  • Heavy Bolter (from the Boltgun) is not Heavy Bolter Fidelis (Morvenn Vahl’s weapon, evolving toward Purgator Mirabilis).
