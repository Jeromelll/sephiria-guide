Warhammer Survivors Weapon Evolutions

The October demo has eleven weapon evolutions in community reports and in a file datamine. One of them, Tempest Axes + Sabaton = Whirlwind Axes, was also played in an IGN preview. This page does not list damage.

Last updated 2026-10-27 · Demo build

🚧 Demo-era build: Warhammer Survivors launches November 10, 2026. A demo is playable now on Steam and Xbox PC — pages are seeded from demo-era sources and completed at launch.

Search pages for “Warhammer Survivors weapon evolutions” are currently a community wiki, a datamine, and a write-up of developer posts. whsurvivors.wiki publishes the October community pairings and says they are unconfirmed. CapyDB prints the same pairings and then adds file stats. The Games Wiki sticks to forms Auroch Digital has described. This page is the pairing table only, with the source of each cell labelled, so it can sit next to those results without pretending a datamine is a patch note.

October demo pairings

Count them as eleven unique recipes. Nuln Oil + Hourglass = Mordant Earth is reported for both Warhammer 40,000 and Age of Sigmar, which is why some tables show twelve rows. The October community post does not give levels. CapyDB’s datamine says each base weapon maxes at 8 and that the passive only has to be in the inventory. Treat that sentence as a datamine. The how evolutions work page keeps the boss-chest reports in one place.

Official weapon facts that are not the pairing

  • Chainsword — Malum Caedo’s starting weapon, horizontal serrated attacks. The Eviscerator result is not in that post.
  • Lasgun — fires at the nearest enemy; levelling it adds Cadian squadmates; taking it on another champion recruits Trooper Kozlowski. Meltagun is the reported evolution, not the official description of those levels.
  • Boltgun — Brother Luca’s starter. The developer says it evolves into a Heavy Bolter with a stream of fire. Dice Cube is the reported companion.
  • Whirlwind Battery — missiles from above in the IGN hands-on. The developer names Orbital Bombardment as the evolution. Liquid Green Stuff is the October reported companion; a May community list said Tape Measure.
  • Nuln Oil — a cross-universe weapon that applies Oiled, so fire damage hurts more. Mordant Earth is the reported evolution. See status effects.
  • Rope and Grapnel — hooks from the screen edge. The developer describes the Ghost of Rhea evolution. White Dwarf is only in the community / datamine column.

Art cards for Chainsword, Lasgun, Whirlwind Axes and Nuln Oil are on the older illustrated evolutions page. The full set is the table on this page.

Do not read a blank effect as zero, and do not invent one. Where the cell says TBD, the evolved weapon’s behaviour has not been published.
