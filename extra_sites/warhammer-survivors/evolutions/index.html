Warhammer Survivors Evolutions: Recipes, Mechanics & Lists

Weapons evolve by combining with a power-up. The Steam page states that rule. Every October demo pairing on this hub is a community report or a file datamine, not an official recipe list.

Last updated 2026-10-27 · Demo build

🚧 Demo-era build: Warhammer Survivors launches November 10, 2026. A demo is playable now on Steam and Xbox PC — pages are seeded from demo-era sources and completed at launch.

Warhammer Survivors evolutions are a weapon plus one specific companion, becoming a new form. That is the mechanic on the Steam page. It is not “upgrade any weapon three times.” IGN’s 6 October 2026 preview is the only public hands-on of a finished pairing: Neave Blacktalon’s Tempest Axes become Whirlwind Axes after Sabaton is levelled. IGN also said the requirement is unclear in the game, which is why the recipes are written down here.

The wider October table — eleven unique pairings — comes from one Steam community report, kept at whsurvivors.wiki, and from CapyDB, which says it read the same pairings out of demo files. The Games Wiki lists developer-described evolved forms (Heavy Bolter, Orbital Bombardment, Zangrom-Thaz, Gauntlets of Ultramar) without treating the demo companion table as official. This hub follows that split. Demo recipes are labelled community or datamine. Damage, cooldown and area numbers from those datamines are not copied onto this site.

Eleven unique recipes. Nuln Oil + Hourglass is the same pairing reported in both universes, so some lists count twelve rows. Required levels are not in the October community post. CapyDB’s datamine claims a weapon max level of 8 and says the passive need not be maxed. That claim is a datamine. Confirm it at launch.

Announced forms with no published passive

  • Zangrom-Thaz — evolving the Fyrestorm Greataxe calls Gotrek Gurnisson. Passive: confirm at launch / TBD. Not in the eleven-row demo table.
  • Gauntlets of Ultramar — evolving the Power Fists calls Marneus Calgar, who fires bolter rounds with every attack. Passive: TBD.
  • Purgator Mirabilis — the Heavy Bolter Fidelis evolution described on PlayStation.Blog, unlocking a missile launcher. Passive: TBD.
