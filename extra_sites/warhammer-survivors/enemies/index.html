Warhammer Survivors Enemies Codex

Every enemy named so far, grouped by faction, with developer-described skills, stage and tier.

🚧 Last updated 2026-10-24 · Demo build. Warhammer Survivors launches November 10, 2026. Every entry cites its source; nothing here is datamined or guessed. Missing values are marked Pending launch.
Codex homeCharactersWeapons & ItemsEnemies

Each stage has its own enemy faction (Steam page), each faction has its own Extremis Boss (April 9 announcement), and factions carry tabletop-inspired Faction Traits such as the Tyranid Synapse and the Ork Waaagh!. The Hobby Table stage mixes several factions from the same universe.

How tiers are assigned here: Boss = described as a boss or Extremis Boss; Elite = given a developer-described special skill or role; Normal = named as rank-and-file. The game’s own classification is pending launch.
