Warhammer Survivors Weapons & Items Codex

Announced weapons, their evolutions and companion passives, plus pick-ups — official, press and community-reported entries kept apart.

🚧 Last updated 2026-10-24 · Demo build. Warhammer Survivors launches November 10, 2026. Every entry cites its source; nothing here is datamined or guessed. Missing values are marked Pending launch.

The Steam page promises an arsenal of 75+ weapons plus passives and power-ups; weapons combine with a specific passive to evolve. Some weapons are cross-universe (Nuln Oil, D6 Dice). Pick-ups are temporary on-field items that differ per universe. Recipe cards with art live on the weapon evolutions page.
