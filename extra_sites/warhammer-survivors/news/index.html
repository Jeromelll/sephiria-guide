Sep 17, 2026

Feature Focus: Faction Traits

  • Faction Traits are tabletop-inspired mechanics that affect each enemy faction differently
  • Tyranid Synapse creatures such as the Hive Tyrant give nearby Termagants and Hormagaunts Berserk
  • A red-tinted screen signals an Ork Waaagh!: more Orks spawn and they become frenzied

Sep 3, 2026

Feature Focus: Enemy Skills

  • Enemy skills let enemies move unpredictably, charge suddenly, heal, or flee and return
  • Von Ryan's Leaper has a Pounce ability, leaping over enemies to impale the player
  • The Bell of Doom grants nearby Skaven the Warded status effect, making them tougher

Aug 27, 2026

Pixelising Champions and horrors from the Warhammer universes

  • Lead Concept Artist Dom and Concept Artist Ohm discuss creating character, enemy and weapon sprites
  • Artists identify key design features from references, then simplify them into small identifiable sprites
  • Each enemy is largely limited to one movement animation, such as the Bomb Squig's few-frame loop

Aug 19, 2026

Feature Focus: Pick-ups

  • Pick-ups appear around faction-themed light sources and mostly differ by universe
  • 40K: Melta-bomb, Blast Template screen-clear and Hellfire Ammo (ranged damage up, applies Poisoned)
  • AoS Endless Spells: Purple Sun of Shyish, Ravenak's Gnashing Jaws (Burning) and Suffocating Gravetide (stun)

Aug 12, 2026

Creating Concept Art for Warhammer Survivors

  • New blog series on how the art team created Warhammer Survivors, starting with concept art
  • Artists explore character, weapon and enemy sprites and refine them with team feedback
  • Water Witch Studio and RocketBrush helped with stages, characters, enemies and VFX

Aug 5, 2026

Feature Focus: Status Effects

  • Burning increases the movement speed of ablaze enemies while Poisoned slows them
  • Oiled, caused by the Nuln Oil weapon, increases damage from weapons that inflict fire damage
  • The Frenzon pick-up imbues the player with Berserk, increasing weapon attack frequency

Hang-out at the Hobby Table

  • The Hobby Table stage exists in both the 40K and Age of Sigmar universes

  • Any character from that universe can be picked; more flourishes are planned for launch

Learn more about the Status Effects system

  • Burning inflicts fire damage over time and speeds up affected enemies
  • Poisoned inflicts poison damage over time and slows enemies down
  • Stunned temporarily halts enemy movement but not their attacks
