Warhammer Survivors Stages & Maps

Officially described settings and preview-reported stages, with demo availability kept separate.

Last updated 2026-10-26 · Demo build

🚧 Demo-era build: The public sources name or show these five stages, but do not establish that all are available in the October demo.

The Steam page describes multiple modes and stages but does not give a complete map list. IGN preview cites 15+ stages as full-game scope; that is not a verified launch-by-launch map list or a demo stage count.

Five named stages

Imperial Bastion

Warhammer 40,000. Defend the remnants of an Imperial Bastion from Tyranid swarms.

Availability: Auroch’s May 21 first-demo announcement names it as a playable demo scenario. October-demo availability not separately confirmed.

The Great Parch

Age of Sigmar; Aqshy. Neave Blacktalon fights Skaven across the ashen plains.

Availability: Auroch’s May 21 first-demo announcement names it as a playable demo scenario. October-demo availability not separately confirmed.

Hammerhal

Age of Sigmar; Aqshy and Ghyran. City under siege by Skaven and Blades of Khorne; the Stormrift Realmgate connects biomes and enemy factions.

Availability: Officially revealed by Auroch in May. Current October-demo availability unknown.

Gallowdark

Warhammer 40,000; abandoned Space Hulk. Tight, corrupted corridors traversed by Death Guard.

Availability: Officially revealed by Auroch in May. Current October-demo availability unknown.

The Hobby Table

Cross-faction tabletop setting. Paint pots, cutting mats and hobby tools form the arena; mixed enemy factions appear.

Availability: Officially revealed by Auroch in May and described in an October IGN preview. Whether that preview establishes access in the current October demo is unclear.

What remains unknown

The sources do not give a complete stage roster, a verified October-demo map selection, or stage unlock routes. Do not infer current-demo access from a May reveal or a full-game preview.
