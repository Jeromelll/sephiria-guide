Warhammer Survivors Stages & Maps

Officially described settings and preview-reported stages, with demo availability kept separate.

Last updated 2026-10-24 · Demo build

🚧 Demo-era build: Warhammer Survivors launches November 10, 2026. A demo is playable now on Steam and Xbox PC — pages are seeded from demo-era sources and completed at launch.

The full game ships with 15+ stages (IGN preview). This preview figure is not a verified map-by-map launch list or a count of playable demo maps.

Settings described by the developer

The official Steam page names the open, ashen plains of Aqshy and the confined corridors of an abandoned Space Hulk. It describes stages with their own enemy factions and secrets, but does not give demo unlock conditions for these settings.

The Hobby TableCrossoverBuilt from paint pots and hobby tools with mixed factions from the same universe

Why the Hobby Table matters

It is the stage that ties the game to the actual Warhammer hobby — a tabletop battlefield assembled from modelling supplies, mixing factions that would never share a battlefield in lore. It also ties into the paint pot collectible, which unlocks character forms and secrets.

What we do not know yet

The table describes public previews, not a confirmed October demo map selection. Hobby Table demo access and individual stage unlocks have not been verified here. Stage routes require checks in a named game build.
