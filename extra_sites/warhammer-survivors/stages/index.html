Warhammer Survivors Maps & Stages

Officially described settings, stages previews have shown, and the changes patch 2.1.8 made to three of them.

Last updated 2026-11-27 · Launch build (patch 2.1.8)

✅ Out now — 10 November 2026. Patch 2.1.8 is live on Steam (25 November 2026): balance pass, miniboss health bars, new grey Skull Gem. Full update log

The game ships with 15+ stages (IGN preview). Stage-by-stage bosses and factions are listed below; launch-build order for stages not yet verified is marked.

Settings described by the developer

The official Steam page names the open, ashen plains of Aqshy and the confined corridors of an abandoned Space Hulk. It describes stages with their own enemy factions and secrets, but does not give demo unlock conditions for these settings.

Stages shown in previews

StageSettingWhat is distinctive
Tyranid battlefieldWarhammer 40,000Tyranid-infested ground — the 40K horde showcase

The Hobby TableCrossoverBuilt from paint pots and hobby tools with mixed factions from the same universe

Why the Hobby Table matters

It is the stage that ties the game to the actual Warhammer hobby — a tabletop battlefield assembled from modelling supplies, mixing factions that would never share a battlefield in lore. It also ties into the paint pot collectible, which unlocks character forms and secrets.

What patch 2.1.8 changed on 25 November 2026

StageChange
GallowdarkCorridors widened; the first miniboss is now a Nurgling

Source: Hotfix v2.1.8 announcement, 25 November 2026. The same patch lowered the maximum health of several enemies across the Death Guard, Tyranids and Orks, and added health bars to minibosses — see the enemies codex.

What we do not know yet

The table describes public previews. Individual stage unlocks, the full stage list and stage routes have not been verified here; they need checks in the named launch build (patch 2.1.8).
